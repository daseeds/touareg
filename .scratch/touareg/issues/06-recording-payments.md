## Question

The app records Payments but never moves money. Settle how a Payment gets in, and what happens to the
Members who have not paid:

- Who records a Payment — the Owner entering what they received, or each Member declaring they have
  paid? Full transparency means everyone can see every Balance, which bears on this.
- What does a Payment cover: a whole Fare year's Dues, a part payment, or a chosen set of Expenses?
- What does a Balance have to be for a Member to count as settled? Exactly zero, or is "Dues paid,
  expense shares outstanding" acceptable for the year?
- Chasing. Does the app do anything, or is chasing entirely manual — the Owner looking at a list of
  who is behind? If the app surfaces it, to whom: the Owner only, the Member themselves, or everyone?
- Does a Payment need evidence attached: a bank transaction reference, a photo of the transfer, a
  free-text note?
- Is there ever a refund, an overpayment, or a negative Payment?
- Does recording a Payment that takes a Balance negative create a credit owed back to that Member by
  the others, and if so is that the same mechanism as out-of-pocket Expense credit?
