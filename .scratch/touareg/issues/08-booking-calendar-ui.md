## Question

Build a cheap, rough artifact of the booking surface and react to it. Half-day Slots,
first-come-first-served, free cancellation, a per-Member cap.

Settle by looking at it rather than by arguing about it:

- The core view. A month grid with three Slot rows per day, or a week timeline? What does a Member see
  at a glance without opening anything?
- How does someone claim a run of consecutive Slots without a dozen separate clicks? Drag? A start and
  end? One click per Slot?
- How is availability communicated — empty, booked and by whom, in the past, or blocked because the boat
  is in the yard?
- Where does a Member mark a Booking Completed, and is that the same screen they booked from?
- How does the per-Member cap surface at the point of booking rather than as a rejection after the fact?
- Mobile: does the same grid work at phone width, or does the phone need a genuinely different layout?
  The app is used on both, and this is where that usually breaks.
