## Question

Settle how the money is actually computed. Share is 1 for every Member unless overridden; the Fare
year is the calendar year; mid-year joiners are prorated; every Expense is divided among Members by
Share; and when a Member fronts a cost out of pocket they are credited for it.

- Is a Share an absolute weight or a fraction? If the Owner holds 2 and there are 4 Members, does the
  Expense divide into 6 parts and the Owner take two, or is the Owner charged a flat 2/6 of it?
- Proration: by months remaining, by days, or by a flat half-year rule? And when a Member leaves
  mid-year, are their Dues refunded, and does their Share change for the rest of the year?
- Does a Share override apply for the whole Fare year, or only to Expenses raised during it? A Member
  who joins in October with a lower Share complicates earlier Expenses.
- When the Owner fronts a cost, do the other Members owe the Owner as a person, or is it simply added
  to each Balance and settled through the bank? The glossary says Balance; confirm the wording, since
  it decides whether the app models interpersonal debt.
- Rounding: Expenses rarely divide evenly. Round each Member's share to the nearest cent, round up, or
  round down — and does the remainder get absorbed onto the Expense so the parts sum to the whole?
- Can an Expense dated to a closed Fare year still be edited or added? If not, what is the correction
  path for a bill that arrives in March for last season?
