## Question

Days Used (Completed Bookings) and Days Booked (Bookings that were not Cancelled) are recorded per
Member. Settle what is actually reported:

- Which figures get displayed? Per-Member Days Used for the Fare year; a total across Members; a month
  by month breakdown; Slots-used as well as days-used?
- The unit. Slots, half-days, or converted days where three Slots is one and a half days? Pick one for
  display and say how it converts.
- Are Days Booked and Days Used shown side by side? A Member with many Bookings and few Completions
  looks bad, and the gap may be entirely innocent — it may just mean nobody got round to tapping.
- What is shown while a Booking is neither Completed nor Cancelled? It counts as booked, forever, unless
  something closes it. Does anything close past Bookings automatically, and if so does the app invent a
  Completion it cannot know happened?
- Given full transparency was chosen, is there a fairness view — who has used the boat most — or does
  displaying a leaderboard invite the comparison it is meant to settle? _Settled in Booking rules: yes,
  and it is the whole mechanism, since the fairness cap was dropped and nothing is enforced. It shows two
  figures per Member — days used in the current Fare year, and days used over the rolling last six
  months._ Settle how they are laid out, and whether Days Booked gets the same two figures or stays
  Fare-year only.
- Lifetime totals per Member, or only the two windows above?
