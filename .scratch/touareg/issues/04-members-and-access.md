## Question

The Owner invites by email, each Member sets a password, and the invite list is the roster. Settle:

- Does a Member exist in the app before they accept their invite — so the Owner can assign a Share and
  set Dues up front — or only once they have accepted?
- Owner is a role a Member holds. Can more than one Member be Owner, and can it be transferred? If the
  Owner steps down, what happens to administrative rights?
- The roster is also the set of people an Expense is divided among. If a Member leaves, are they
  removed from the split for future Expenses while keeping their history, and do they keep read access
  to the app at all?
- Can a Share or a set of Dues be changed retrospectively once Expenses have been recorded against
  them, or is the Fare year sealed?
- Are Dues ever anything other than one flat amount for everyone? Variation is supposed to be expressed
  through Share alone — confirm that, because it is what keeps the ledger arithmetically simple.
- What does an invite look like when it expires, is resent, or goes to the wrong address?
