## Question

When work on the boat is paid for, it has to reach the ledger. Settle the seam between the two
subsystems:

- Who pays for a repair — the Owner out of pocket, the boat's bank account, or a Member fronting it? If
  the answer differs by case, does the app model those differently, or does the Expense record who paid
  and treat that as bookkeeping detail?
- When an Expense is raised against a Maintenance Item, is the link one-way (the Expense points at the
  Item) or must the Item carry the cost itself?
- Can one Expense cover several Maintenance Items — a haul-out that fixes three things at once? Can one
  Item take several Expenses across months, as a long-running repair does?
- Once the Expense for an Item is paid, does the Item become done automatically, or does someone still
  have to mark it done? An Item whose work is paid for but not recorded as finished is how maintenance
  lists rot.
- Is there work that is paid for but was never a Maintenance Item — routine fuel, insurance, mooring,
  berth fees — and how is that kept distinguishable from a repair?
- Should the Maintenance Items list be able to show cost, so a Member can see what keeping the boat
  afloat actually costs, or is the finance side deliberately separate from the to-do side?
